About Kulan Cyber Advisory

Strategic Architects of Institutional Trust

Kulan Cyber Advisory provides elite GRC and Emerging Technology Advisory to financial institutions, telecoms, and healthcare organizations across regulated markets. We do not perform utility-level technical work — we serve as the strategic architect for institutional compliance.

Our Approach

We diagnose, remediate, and validate an institution's compliance posture before the next inspection window opens — using the same three-phase methodology on every engagement, documented in full on our Frameworks page.

Our model is fully remote and digital-first, which means the same standard of rigor that wins in East Africa scales directly into North America and Europe — from CMMC-readiness for US government contractors to AI privacy compliance for global startups.
